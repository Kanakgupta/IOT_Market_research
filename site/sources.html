Sources & Methodology

Everything on this site comes from publicly available sources. Every item links back to its origin.

How the agent works (nightly, unattended)

  1. Search. Runs a fixed set of public news queries for every tracked company, application and industry topic in Google News RSS (US, UK, Germany, India, China, Taiwan and Japan editions) and Bing News RSS. China-based vendors are also searched in Chinese.
  2. Relevance guard. A company story is kept only when the company is named in the headline. Ambiguous brand names (e.g. “Ring”, “Nothing”, “Honor”) are only matched within the company's own targeted query.
  3. De-duplicate & cluster. The same story syndicated by several outlets becomes one item with “+N more outlets” links, so coverage breadth becomes a signal.
  4. Classify. Each headline is tagged by technology (Wi-Fi / Bluetooth / UWB-Matter-NearLink / business), by theme (Wi-Fi 7/8, LE Audio, Channel Sounding, M&A, launches, earnings, regulation…) and by region (company HQ, news edition, publisher country).
  5. Score. Importance = recency + theme weight (M&A, Wi-Fi 8 and partnerships rank higher) + outlet breadth + tier-1 publisher + wireless relevance.
  6. Market forecasts. Public market-size press releases are parsed for value, target year and CAGR. A forecast only counts when the headline names the application's market. The 10-year curve uses the median of the evidence, and outliers are excluded and flagged. When no public forecast is found, the chart says so and shows an indicative index instead.
  7. Publish. Static data files are written for this website. No server is needed, and the site can be hosted anywhere.

Accuracy & usage notes

  • Company profiles (business model, product families) cover publicly documented business lines, linked to each official website. Anything time-sensitive comes from dated, linked news.
  • Classification is rule-based and deterministic, so it is reproducible. Open the source link before acting on any single headline.
  • Market-research press releases vary in scope and quality. That is why the site shows every data point, its source, and whether it was used.
  • Google News links open through Google's redirect to the original publisher. Bing items link directly to the publisher.
  • The RSS feeds are licensed for personal, non-commercial feed-reader use. For commercial or public redistribution, switch to a licensed news API (for example Bing News Search API, NewsAPI or GDELT). Only the fetcher would need to change.
